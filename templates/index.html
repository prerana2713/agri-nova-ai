          Application Information & Project Disclaimer

          Agri Nova AI — Flask + SQLAlchemy + Scikit-learn Random Forest + ReportLab QR PDF Generator. Model trained on a 99-record educational dataset covering 6 crops (Rice, Wheat, Maize, Cotton, Sugarcane, Groundnut), 5 soil types (Sandy, Loamy, Black, Red, Clayey), and 7 fertilizer classes (Urea, DAP, 10-26-26, 14-35-14, 17-17-17, 20-20, 28-28).

          Academic Prototype Disclaimer: This application is intended strictly for academic demonstration, hackathons, and research exploration. Predictions and suitability scores are not validated agricultural advice and must not replace certified soil laboratory testing or consultation with a qualified agronomist.
